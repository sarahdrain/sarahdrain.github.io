Job Market Paper

The Cost of Care Substitution: Nurse Practitioner Autonomy and Medicare Spending

Examines whether permanent nurse practitioner full practice authority changes Medicare billing participation, claim-recorded nurse practitioner and physician service allocation, utilization, and standardized spending.

The study uses staggered state adoption and public Medicare Geographic Variation, Provider, and Provider-Service data alongside AHRF/NPPES records. It distinguishes expanded legal authority from observed billing activity, recognizing that incident-to billing can attribute nurse practitioner–furnished care to physicians.
